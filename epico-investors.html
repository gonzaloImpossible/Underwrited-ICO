Mechanism

What happens to your money

At ICO close / TGE
Your funds enter Impossible Finance escrow. Tokens are minted but not in your wallet yet. You can claim them all immediately — but doing so permanently waives your refund right. Most investors will hold.
Every month (1–10)
Three things settle on-chain:

① Airdrop — you automatically receive 10% of your tokens.

② Team release — 10% of your deposit transfers from escrow to the team.

③ Refund window — if you opted to refund, you receive your protected balance.
Your choice — anytime
Between settlements you can always:

Hold — do nothing. Receive the next airdrop, refund right ticks down 10%.

Claim — receive all remaining tokens immediately. Refund right waived permanently.

Refund — return your unissued tokens, recover the corresponding escrow balance.
After month 10
You've received all your tokens. The escrow is empty. The team is fully funded. Your refund right has expired naturally — by then, the project has had 10 months to prove itself.
