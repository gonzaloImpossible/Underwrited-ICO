The Solution

Earn your capital. Win the right buyers.

Raised funds enter an Impossible Finance escrow and are released to you in 10 monthly tranches — tied to non-refunded investor balances. You receive capital as you keep your investor base.

Investors hold a declining refund right against their unclaimed tokens, fully backed by the escrow. They can exit if you miss — but the right decays linearly to zero over 10 months.

The structure itself is the signal. A team planning to rug wouldn't accept escrow. Teams that do accept it self-identify as serious — and get priced accordingly.

Bilateral commitment
Investors commit capital for 10 months — every month they don't refund extends your runway and proves traction.

You commit to monthly delivery — every month you ship is a month the refund right decays in your favour.

Both sides are tied to outcomes, not just to each other on day one.

How It Works

Capital, delivered monthly

At ICO close / TGE
Investor funds enter Impossible Finance escrow. Your tokens are minted per your tokenomics and the public sale allocation enters the protected pool. You receive nothing yet. Investors can claim their tokens immediately — but doing so waives their refund right.
Every month (1–10)
Three settlements happen on-chain:

① Team tranche — you receive 10% of every non-refunded investor's deposit.

② Airdrop — holders receive 10% of their tokens automatically.

③ Refund window — any investor who opted out receives their protected balance.
Refunds are funded — never on you
Refunds come from escrow, not from your treasury. You can't be drained, sued, or chased. The escrow contract is structurally solvent for every possible refund scenario. Your only exposure is the capital you would have received from that investor — never more.
Month 10 — fully settled
All tokens delivered to holders. Escrow at zero. You've received the full residual raise from every investor who chose to stay. Refund right expired naturally — by design.

TGE Price Defense

Your launch holds — not crashes

Standard ICO at TGE

  • → Every holder is unlocked at the same moment with paper gains
  • → First sellers take the best price — racing to exit is rational
  • → Initial price collapse signals weakness, triggering more selling
  • → Your narrative becomes "another dump" in week one

EPICO at TGE

  • → Claiming to sell forfeits the 100% refund right — there's a real cost to dumping
  • → Rational investors hold the option rather than throw it away
  • → Reduced supply at launch supports your price — no cascade
  • → You enter price discovery from a position of strength
Engineered, not enforced
Lockups suppress sell pressure by force — and create a known dump date everyone front-runs. EPICO suppresses sell pressure through rational incentive: investors don't dump because the option to refund is worth more than the marginal upside of selling early.

Result: a launch that holds, instead of a launch that explodes and re-builds.

EPICO turns your ICO buyers from short-term traders into option-holders. The buyers who'd have dumped at TGE now hold by choice — and your launch price reflects that.

Scope

What EPICO governs — and what stays yours

What EPICO governs
Only the public sale allocation — the tokens and capital from the ICO tranche routed through Impossible Finance.

Inside this scope: escrow, monthly release, airdrop, refund right. The structure is fixed and audited.
What stays under your control
Every other allocation in your tokenomics:

• Team and advisor vesting
• Treasury and ecosystem funds
• LP provisioning
• Strategic partnerships
• Future emissions and incentives

No constraints outside the ICO tranche. Your treasury, your call.

EPICO is a raise structure, not a full-stack governance system. You commit to escrow on the public sale only — and keep complete control over the rest of your tokenomics, your treasury, and your operations.
