The Problem

Token Raises Start With A Credibility Deficit

"Equity gives buyers legal rights. Tokens don't. Industry practices have spent the trust. Promises don't price into a raise anymore."

No Legal Floor Like Equity

Equity buyers get shareholder rights, clawbacks, and legal recourse if the team underdelivers. Token buyers have none of that. Every ICO is purchased on trust alone — there is no statutory backstop investors can fall back on.

No Recourse

Trust Has Been Spent

Years of rugs, vested-then-dumped unlocks, and silent pivots have hardened investor skepticism. Audits, multisigs, vesting schedules — all priced in and discounted. The marginal investor no longer takes promises at face value.

Promises Don't Price

Credibility Takes Time

Teams that consistently ship and accrue value to the token do earn recognition — but it takes 12+ months of visible delivery. At launch you have no track record. You need a way to earn trust as you build, not just claim it on day one.

Need Time To Prove

The teams who deliver get rewarded eventually — but you need to raise capital and survive long enough to prove it. EPICO is the bridge: investors fund you, you demonstrate execution on-chain every month, and credibility compounds on the calendar instead of waiting on belief at TGE.

How It Works

Capital In Escrow, Delivered As Trust Is Earned

"Raised funds sit in Impossible Finance escrow — fully backing every refund right. You receive 10% each month, as the calendar passes and investors stay."

At TGE — Funds Enter Escrow

Investor funds land in Impossible Finance escrow on day one — never in your treasury. Your tokens are minted and the public-sale allocation enters the protected pool. You receive nothing yet.

Each Month — 10% Streams To You

10% of every non-refunded deposit transfers from escrow to you. Investors receive 10% of their tokens. Their refund right ticks down 10%. Three settlements, on-chain, every month.

Refunds Are Funded — Never On You

If an investor refunds, they're paid from escrow — not your treasury. You can't be drained, sued, or chased. Your only exposure is the capital you would have received from that investor.

Capital Follows Execution

At every moment, the escrow holds at least the total outstanding refund rights. As the calendar passes and investors hold, escrow drains in your favor.
